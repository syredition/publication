What's New in New York Art? What's New in New York Art?

With critic and researcher Danielle Jackson, Ben Davis takes the temperature of New York's summer art scene through two exhibitions: Greater New York at MoMA PS1 (53+ artists and collectives, opened April 10, closes August 17) and the Seventh AIM Biennial "Forms of Connection" at the Bronx Museum of the Arts. The AIM (Artist in the Marketplace) Biennial showcases graduates of the museum's year-long career development programme for New York-based artists. The episode tests Josh Kline's provocation — that New York is no longer viable for ambitious new work — against what these two exhibitions actually demonstrate.

AI in Galleries — First Thursday Report AI in Galleries — First Thursday Report

갤러리·컬렉터 관계 플랫폼 First Thursday 창업자 Callum Hale-Thomson이 최신 AI in Galleries Report를 공개합니다. 핵심 발견: 갤러리 전문가들의 AI 도입률이 예상보다 높으며, 이메일 작성·콘텐츠 생산·데이터 정리 등 루틴 업무 중심입니다. 주요 우려 사항은 고객 기밀 데이터 보안과 직원들의 업무 변화 불안이며, AI 직업 대체 공포는 아트 세계에서 타 산업에 비해 낮은 편입니다. Callum Hale-Thomson, founder of First Thursday — a platform helping galleries convert collector relationships into sales — presents findings from the new AI in Galleries Report. Key finding: AI adoption among gallery professionals is significantly higher than expected, concentrated in routine tasks such as email drafting, content creation, and data management. Primary concerns are client data confidentiality and staff anxiety around changing workflows. Fear of AI job displacement is notably lower in the art sector than in other industries.

Re-Air: The Magic of 'The Artist's Way' Re-Air: The Magic of 'The Artist's Way'

1992년 초판 출간 이후 전 세계 스터디 그룹이 생길 만큼 영향력이 지속되는 『아티스트 웨이』 저자 Julia Cameron과의 재방송입니다. Cameron은 창작을 타고나는 능력이 아닌 회복될 수 있는 것으로 정의하고, 매일 아침 손으로 쓰는 '모닝 페이지(3페이지)'와 주 1회 '아티스트 데이트'를 핵심 방법론으로 제시합니다. Ben Davis는 이 인터뷰 준비를 위해 모닝 페이지를 시작했고 6개월이 지난 지금도 계속하고 있다고 밝혔습니다. Cameron의 신간 The Daily Artist's Way(Macmillan)도 소개됩니다. A re-air of Ben Davis's interview with Julia Cameron, author of The Artist's Way (1992) — a book that continues to generate study groups worldwide and find new readers. Cameron defines creativity not as innate talent but as something that can be recovered, through daily Morning Pages (three handwritten pages each morning) and weekly Artist Dates. Davis started Morning Pages to prepare for the original interview and was still doing them six months later. Cameron's new book, The Daily Artist's Way (Macmillan), is also discussed.

Jul. 24 ArtReview
Podcast · Ep. 09
Iain Forsyth + Jane Pollard + Jack White

Episode 9: Jack White — "These Thoughts May Disappear" Episode 9: Jack White — "These Thoughts May Disappear"

뮤지션 Jack White의 첫 공식 갤러리 전시 These Thoughts May Disappear(Newport Street Gallery, 런던, 9월까지)에 맞춰 제작된 에피소드입니다. ArtReview 팟캐스트의 포맷은 게스트가 자신의 실천을 탐구하는 렌즈로 세 가지 작품을 직접 고릅니다. White는 디트로이트, 실내 장식(upholstery), 실수(mistakes)를 선택했습니다. 음악에서의 실수(재녹음)와 시각 예술에서의 실수(작품 안에 영구히 남음)의 차이를 어떻게 실천에 통합했는지가 핵심 논점입니다. Produced to coincide with Jack White's first formal gallery exhibition, These Thoughts May Disappear at Newport Street Gallery, London (until September). ArtReview's podcast format has guests choose three artworks as lenses for their practice. White chose Detroit, upholstery, and mistakes. The core discussion: how the fundamental difference between mistakes in music (retake possible) and mistakes in visual art (permanently embedded in the work) shaped his visual practice. Hosts Iain Forsyth and Jane Pollard are ArtReview contributors.
